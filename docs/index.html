About Me

would like to briefly introduce myself.

I have recently completed an MSci in Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence) at Royal Holloway, University of London, and I am currently waiting for my final results. During my studies, I developed a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Analysis, and Software Engineering. I enjoy learning new technologies and applying them to solve real-world problems.

During my degree, I completed a research placement focused on high-dimensional data visualisation and dimensionality reduction using techniques such as PCA and t-SNE. This experience gave me the opportunity to work closely with academic researchers, improve my Python programming skills, and gain experience analysing complex datasets. It also strengthened my ability to learn independently and work on research-based projects.

For my final-year project, I developed and evaluated deep learning models for medical image segmentation using Attention U-Net architectures. Throughout my studies, I also completed several software engineering and machine learning projects using Python, Java, SQL, PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Git. These projects helped me develop strong problem-solving skills, experience working in Agile teams, and confidence in designing, implementing, and testing software.

I am always looking for opportunities to continue learning, improve my technical skills, and contribute to meaningful projects in Artificial Intelligence and Software Engineering.
